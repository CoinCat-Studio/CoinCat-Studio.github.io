THE ARENA

Cold steel.
Hot blood.

Beneath a blood-red sky stands the great arena. Kings watch from the stands. Fighters enter in chains and leave as legends, or never leave at all.

In GLAUDIRIUS you train, gear up and step onto the sand, one duel at a time. Win, and the crowd chants your name. Lose, and the arena forgets you.
